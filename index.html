Vision

Lighting the Path to Knowledge

Inspired by the torch and open book in our emblem, we believe every child carries a light that education must kindle. Our approach blends academic rigour with warmth, creativity, and character formation.

Our Mission

To provide a joyful, disciplined, and inclusive learning environment that empowers each child to discover their strengths and contribute meaningfully.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of holistic education, where knowledge becomes light and every learner grows into a confident, compassionate global citizen.

Founder

Marhoom Ghulam Mohammad Khan
(Sharief Sahab)

Founder & Visionary Thinker
Former JKAS Officer & Former Director Finance

My father dedicated his life to education, but what made him truly special was the kindness and sincerity with which he guided others.
Wherever life took him, he remained a teacher at heart.
He gave his time to students, listened to them, encouraged them, and helped them believe in themselves.
He never measured his work by what he received in return.
He lived simply, served quietly, and left goodness wherever he went.
Imam Jaʿfar al Sadiq (a.s.) said:
“One who teaches something good to someone will receive a reward like that of the one who acts upon it, even if he dies.”
I cannot think of words that describe my father better.
His students remain the living memory of a life spent in the service of knowledge.

Muntazir Mehdi

Academics

A Journey of Growth

From playful beginnings to confident middle school years.

Early Years

Nursery · LKG · UKG

Age: 3–6 years

Approach: Play-based, sensory, and social learning.

Focus: Language, numeracy, motor skills, creativity.

Primary School

Class 1 – 5

Age: 6–11 years

Approach: Concept-driven, activity-supported.

Core Subjects: ELGA, Maths, EVS, Hindi, Coding, Urdu, Arts.

Focus: Critical thinking, reading, collaboration.

Middle School

Class 6 – 8

Age: 11–14 years

Approach: Inquiry-based, subject-specialised.

Core Subjects: English, Maths, Science, Social Science, Hindi, Computers, Urdu.

Focus: Analytical skills, research, leadership.
